Claw Spot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Minsky Games Workshop

This page mirrors the in-app Privacy policy. The complete text also lives inside Claw Spot under Settings → Privacy policy.

Who we are

Claw Spot is made by Minsky Games Workshop. This page is the full privacy policy and short terms for the app. You can read everything here without leaving Claw Spot.

Data on your device

Game progress, coins, settings, stars, and companion care stay on this device only. There is no Claw Spot account and no cloud sync. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data deletes that local data.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, or phone to play. We do not run a Claw Spot login or player database. Optional feedback you send by email is only what you choose to write.

Ads

The app may show ads through Google AdMob (rewarded, interstitial, and banner). Google and its partners may process device and ad identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, and interaction data to deliver and measure ads, subject to applicable law and your choices. Use Ad privacy choices at the bottom of this page when that form is available on your device.

Purchases

A one-time purchase on Google Play or the App Store removes the banner and the ad between levels. The store processes the payment. Claw Spot only records on this device that those forced ads are removed. Watch-ad buttons for hints, pet power, coins, and continue stay optional. Use Restore purchases in Settings after you reinstall.

Children

Claw Spot is a casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for a Claw Spot account. Use parental controls on the device if you need them.

Your choices

You can reset local progress by clearing app data. You can change ad-related privacy choices when the form is available on your device. You can also limit ad tracking in your device system settings.

Short terms of use

Claw Spot is provided for personal entertainment. Progress and stars are stored only on your device. Ads help keep the game available. Do not misuse the app or attempt to interfere with other players' devices. We may update this policy; the date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
